AURELIO — GOOGLE FLOW PROMPTS FOR THE 12 STARTING FRAMES
Frames: output/frames. Use each image as the starting frame and paste its prompt.
Each prompt has its own camera move, so no two clips feel alike. All are cinematic,
slow and smooth, and keep the product exactly as it is in the frame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
01 — white-salon-ray-mirror-v2.png            MOVE: symmetrical dolly-in
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cinematic, photorealistic film of a sunlit white living room. A perfectly centred,
very slow, silky dolly-in glides toward the brass sunburst mirror, with gentle
parallax between the stone table, the mirror and the olive trees beyond the glass.
Fine sand falls steadily through the copper hourglass. A soft breeze stirs the
leaves and the daylight drifts across the rug. The mirror, hourglass and furniture
never change. Calm, grand, true-to-life.
