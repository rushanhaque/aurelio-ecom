====================================================================================================
AURELIO BY AF INTERNATIONAL
HOMEPAGE BACKGROUND FILM: PROMPTS, VERSION 2
Starting-frame image prompts (ChatGPT image generation) and video prompts (Google Flow)
====================================================================================================

11 scenes, 22 complete prompts: 2 indoor scenery, 2 outdoor scenery, 4 single-product and 3
products-in-use. Every scene has one IMAGE PROMPT and one VIDEO PROMPT. Every prompt is complete on
its own: the shared Aurelio direction is written into each one, so you never need to combine
anything. Version 1 (docs/Aurelio_Homepage_Image_and_Video_Prompts.txt) is unchanged; this version
has new scenes and is calibrated to the live homepage layout.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pick a scene. In ChatGPT image generation, attach the product photos listed for it, then paste
   that scene's complete IMAGE PROMPT. Use clean, well-lit product photos; several angles of the
   same product help. Label each photo (for example "pendant light", "side table") when attaching
   more than one.
2. Check the generated image against your product photos before going further. Fix any wrong shape,
   finish, scale, mounting, or (in the in-use scenes) hands. A video inherits every mistake in its
   starting frame, so regenerate or ask for corrections until the still is right.
3. In Google Flow, use Frames to Video. Upload the approved image as the starting frame and paste
   that scene's complete VIDEO PROMPT. Choose landscape 16:9, the highest quality available, and the
   standard length of about 8 seconds.
4. Generate two to four takes of each scene and keep the steadiest one: products unchanged, no
   warping, calm motion. Prompts improve the odds but cannot guarantee exact product fidelity, so
   always check every take.
5. Seamless loop (optional): if Flow lets you set an ending frame, you can give the same approved
   image as both the first and last frame. That makes a single clip that loops without a jump.
6. Never put the AURELIO name, buttons or any text into the image or film. The website draws those
   on top, and the prompts keep space clear for them.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE THE WEBSITE SITS ON TOP OF THE FILM (measured on the live homepage)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- AURELIO wordmark: about 7-19% down from the top, across the middle third of the width.
- Two buttons: about 85-91% down, across the middle 36% of the width.
- "Contact us": a small tag on the right edge, at mid-height.
- Veil: the site softly darkens the top 30% and bottom 45% of the film with deep emerald.
- Phones: a tall phone screen shows only the central ~26% of a 16:9 film's width. That is why every
  prompt places the hero product on the centre line. For a perfect phone version, see the PORTRAIT
  CUT section at the end.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COMMON DIRECTION (already included in every prompt below)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Brand: a family atelier in Moradabad, since 2008; brass, copper, bronze, patinated steel, blown
  glass and seasoned wood, made by hand.
- Mood: fresh, lively, light and quietly luxurious, like an editorial interiors film. Calm, never
  busy; alive, never static.
- Palette: chalk white, warm ivory #F2EFE6, paper white #F2F2F2, pale limestone and travertine,
  natural light oak, sage and garden greens; small accents of deep emerald #072319 / #0E3B2C; brass
  highlights in champagne #D8C39D / #B9944F.
- Light: soft directional natural daylight, high-key but holding detail; no orange/teal grade, haze,
  sparkle, flare or HDR.
- Products: the attached photos are the truth. Exact shape, finish and scale, rigid and still, never
  redesigned, morphing or duplicated.
- Camera: one slow, stabilised move per clip, about 8 seconds, ending on a held, balanced frame. No
  cuts and no text; works muted.

them; do not invent extra products that compete with them. Any supporting styling is plain,

polished or antiqued brass with restrained champagne highlights (#D8C39D, #B9944F), never a yellow,

vessels on the table and on a simple travertine plinth. One deep emerald velvet cushion is the only
colour accent. Generous space around every piece; a lived-in home, not a showroom.

eye height, with a 35 mm lens character, looking straight down the centre of the room toward the
middle arched window. The seating group and table sit on the centre line in the middle of the frame;
the hanging light is centred above them, below the top band. Late-morning sun falls in soft, long
rectangles across the floor from the left. The upper centre is quiet arched wall and window glow;
the lower centre is clear limestone floor and the edge of the rug.

Frame: landscape 16:9, full-frame cinema-camera character, natural lens perspective, moderate depth
of field with the products crisp and the far background gently soft. This frame will play
full-screen behind the website, so compose for it. Keep a calm, uncluttered band across the top
centre (roughly the top fifth of the frame, across the middle third of the width), where the AURELIO
wordmark will sit, and a calm band across the bottom centre (roughly the lowest fifth, across the
middle two-fifths of the width), where two buttons will sit; plain wall, sky, floor, foliage or
tabletop is ideal there. The website lays a soft deep-emerald veil over the top and bottom edges, so
place the brightest, most important product detail in the middle band of the frame. Phones show only
the central quarter of the frame's width, so place the hero product, or the heart of the
arrangement, on the vertical centre line, inside the central 25 to 30 percent of the width. The
image contains no text, logos, watermarks, borders, letterboxing, collage or split panels: a single
still photograph of the opening moment, with everything physically at rest and free of motion blur.

====================================================================================================
EXPORTING AND ADDING IT TO THE WEBSITE
====================================================================================================

The website is already wired for this film (and, optionally, for a portrait cut). It starts the film
only after the page has loaded, never for visitors who prefer reduced motion, who are saving data or
who are on very slow connections. It pauses the film when it is off screen, and it shows the still
image until the film is actually playing.

1. Export a master at 1920x1080 (or 2560x1440), 24 or 25 fps, with no audio track.
2. Create the two web files (requires ffmpeg), keeping the landscape pair under about 6-8 MB:
     ffmpeg -i master.mp4 -an -vf scale=1920:-2 -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -preset slow -crf 23 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart public/videos/hero.mp4
     ffmpeg -i master.mp4 -an -vf scale=1920:-2 -c:v libvpx-vp9 -b:v 0 -crf 35 -row-mt 1 -deadline good -cpu-used 2 public/videos/hero.webm
3. Optional portrait cut (from a 9:16 master), kept under about 4 MB:
     ffmpeg -i portrait-master.mp4 -an -vf scale=1080:-2 -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -preset slow -crf 24 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart public/videos/hero-portrait.mp4
     ffmpeg -i portrait-master.mp4 -an -vf scale=1080:-2 -c:v libvpx-vp9 -b:v 0 -crf 36 -row-mt 1 -deadline good -cpu-used 2 public/videos/hero-portrait.webm
4. Make the still match the film's first frame, so the film fades in without a visible change:
     ffmpeg -i public/videos/hero.mp4 -frames:v 1 public/images/hero-source.png
     npm run assets
5. Rebuild (npm run build) and deploy. The build detects whichever of hero.mp4, hero.webm,
   hero-portrait.mp4 and hero-portrait.webm exist; when running locally, restart the dev server
   after adding them.
